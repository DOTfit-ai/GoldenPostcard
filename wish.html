Happy Birthday,
My Love.
A letter, written with the whole of my heart

On this quiet and beautiful day, I wish to set down in ink what my voice has not always found the words to say — that you are, without question, the most extraordinary soul I have ever had the privilege of knowing.

Not for what you do, nor for how brightly you shine to the rest of the world — but for the warmth you carry quietly, in the small moments, the ones only I am fortunate enough to witness. The way a room softens when you enter it. The way your laugh arrives before you do.

May this year return to you every ounce of tenderness you have given so freely. May every silent wish you have folded away and kept close finally find its way home to you.

You deserve the whole world — and I intend to spend every remaining day ensuring you feel exactly that.

L
With all of me, faithfully & forever —
Yours, always
